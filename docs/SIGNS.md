# Indian Sign Language (ISL) Verified Signs Reference

SignBridge grounds its vocabulary directly in official Indian Sign Language standards established by the **Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC)** under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.

---

## 1. Official ISL Resources & Attributions
- **ISLRTC 10,000 Sign Terms Dictionary:** https://islrtc.nic.in
- **ISLRTC YouTube Official Channel:** Educational sign corpus demonstrating authentic two-handed syntax and facial markers.
- **Talking Hands (Deaf Enabled Foundation):** Verified conversational vocabulary.

## 3. Important Note on Two-Handed Fingerspelling in ISL
Unlike ASL (which is single-handed for A-Z), **ISL fingerspelling requires both hands**:
- Example **'A'**: Non-dominant index finger points up; dominant index touches its tip.
- Example **'B'**: Both hands form two circles touching to resemble the numeral '8' or letter 'B'.
- SignBridge features an interactive Fingerspelling Aid where users can explore these two-handed shapes accurately without false assumptions.
